Projects

Amazon products analytics dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing Amazon product data across customer satisfaction, product popularity, category performance, pricing and discount strategy. I cleaned and prepared 1,351 products with Power Query, then built the KPIs and visuals that show how ratings, engagement and discounts relate. Key findings: 78% of products are rated above 4, office products are the highest-rated category, and discount size shows no relation to customer engagement or satisfaction.

Tools and skills

Superstore executive and product analytics dashboard

A two-page Power BI dashboard for a retail superstore. The Executive page tracks total sales, profit, profit margin and year-over-year growth, with breakdowns by category, region, year and profitability. The Product page covers the number of products, items sold, best category by profit and average discount, plus monthly orders, sales by segment and a product-level table. All KPI measures were written in DAX. Key findings: sales reached $2.30M with a 12.5% profit margin and 46.9% year-over-year growth, Technology is the most profitable category, and the West is the top-selling region.
